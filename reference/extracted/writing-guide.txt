The PTE Write Essay task requires you to write a 200–300 word academic essay in 20 minutes. Scores drop to zero if you write under 200 words or over 380 words.
Recommended 4-Paragraph Structure
Paragraph 1: Introduction (40–50 words)
Sentence 1: Paraphrase the prompt/topic in your own words.
Sentence 2–3: State your clear opinion or thesis statement (agree/disagree, or outline the main views).
Paragraph 2: Body Paragraph 1 (60–70 words)
Sentence 1: Introduce your first main supporting point.
Sentence 2: Explain or elaborate on the idea.
Sentence 3: Provide a short, relevant example or piece of evidence.
Paragraph 3: Body Paragraph 2 (60–70 words)
Sentence 1: Introduce your second main supporting point (or counter-argument/alternative view).
Sentence 2: Explain or elaborate on this second idea.
Sentence 3: Provide a short example or supporting detail.
Paragraph 4: Conclusion (30–40 words)
Sentence 1: Rephrase your main position and summarize the two key points.
Sentence 2: Give a final concluding thought or closing sentence.
Time Management (20 Minutes Total)
0–2 minutes: Read the prompt and understand the question type (Opinion, Agree/Disagree, Advantages/Disadvantages, or Problem/Solution).
2–5 minutes: Plan your 2 main points and quick vocabulary/linking words.
5–17 minutes: Write the 4 paragraphs (aim for 220–270 words).
17–20 minutes: Proofread for spelling, grammar mistakes, and word count.
Key Rules for a High Score (79+ / 90)
Word Count: Stay strictly between 200 and 300 words (aim around 230–250 words).
Avoid Rigid Templates: Pearson's AI detects overly robotic or heavily memorized templates and may lower your content or structure score; use a flexible, natural framework.
Grammar & Spelling: Keep sentences simple, clear, and grammatically accurate rather than forcing complex structures that cause errors. Limit spelling mistakes to zero or one.

List Words
Firstly
secondly
thirdly
finally / lastly
Example 1 uses listing words to start a sentence.
There are several reasons why I believe cars should be banned from city centers.
Firstly, their exhaust fumes pollute the air, which reduces air quality. Secondly, ... Thirdly, ... Lastly, ...
Example 2 shows how 2 linking words can be used within one sentence.
I believe cars should be banned from city centers because, firstly, they ... and, secondly, they ...
2. Linking Words to add information
These linking words tell the reader you are about to provide more information.
furthermore
moreover
additionally / in addition to
apart from
besides
also
as well as
Examples:
The market research gives us an idea of our target market. Furthermore / Moreover, it tells us who our target customer is.
In addition to our London branch, we have offices in Dubai and Sydney.
Additionally, we can give you a 10% cash discount.
Apart from / Besides Samsung, we are the the most significant television manufacturer in Asia.
Carsin cities generate a lot of noise, as well as air pollution.
3. Linking Words for results and consequences
Use these words to explain the consequences or results of something.
consequently
therefore
as a result
hence
for this reason
Examples:
Using a computer for too long may cause eye strain in children. Consequently, many kids end up wearing glasses.
If the population in cities is increasing too quickly we should, therefore, look at ways to minimize it.
As a result of lack of sports at school, many kids are becoming overweight. For this reason, they need to play games at least twice a week.
4. Linking Words for highlighting and stressing
These words help you to emphasize specific points to give precise meaning.
clearly
obviously
of course
particularly
especially
Examples:
Clearly / obviously, it is not suitable for the natural environment when factories dump toxic waste into nearby rivers.
It is particularly / especially essential for governments to provide public parks and gardens in cities.
When tourists visit small towns and spend money it, of course, benefits local people such as shopkeepers and restaurant owners.
5. Linking Words for giving examples
To support your ideas, you can give examples using these linking words.
for example
for instance
such as
to illustrate
an obvious example is
Examples:
In many countries, high school students are required to do a lot of homework. For example / for instance, in my country, Japan, we usually do 2–3 hours of homework every night.
Unhealthy habits, such as eating junk food and lack of sleep, are becoming increasingly common.
To illustrate why I believe tertiary education is not essential for everyone, I will use my father as an example. He left school at 16 and…
There are many problems related to city living, and an obvious example is…
6. Linking Words for giving your opinion
Use these when you are required to give your opinion.
in my opinion
in my view
I believe
I think
I do not agree
I disagree
Conclusion:
These are generally used in your final paragraph to finish off your essay.
in conclusion
to conclude
to sum up
In conclusion, I believe it is suitable for high school students to have a part-time job as long as it doesn’t interfere with their studies.
To conclude, I think the disadvantages of living in cities are outweighed by the advantages, due to the reasons I have outlined above.
